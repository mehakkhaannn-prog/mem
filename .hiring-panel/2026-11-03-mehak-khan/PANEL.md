## The mistakes, worst first

1. **"Drove 15% increase in hotel reservations and 20% increase in event attendance at Habtoor Hospitality…"** No role, date or employer entry exists for it, and there is no gap in the timeline where it fits. It reads as made up and makes reviewers doubt every other number. *Flagged by: Screen, Hiring Manager, HM day-one, Rival, CEO (5/7).*
2. **"A marketing leader with 10 years of experience across technology, hospitality, and retail in the UAE."** The dates contradict it: the only UAE-marked role starts in 2024 (2022 if Signature is in the UAE), and earlier roles were in the UK, Bahrain and Pakistan. It's an easy credibility check to fail. *Screen, HM, HM day-one, Rival (4/7).*
3. **"The kind who gets into the business first — understanding customers, how they move, what they need — and builds from there."** Informal filler in the most valuable spot on the page, and the summary contains no numbers. *Recruiter, HM, Rival, Screen (4/7).*
4. **The same numbers appear twice** ("78%", "74%", "150K+", traffic) in Key Achievements and again in "Delivered exceptional business results…". It reads as padding, and it shows the whole case rests on one 20-month job. "Exceptional" is self-praise. *Screen, HM, CEO (3/7).*
5. **The current role has no hard results.** "50%+", the range "30-40%", and the CRM/journey bullets have no outcome. Nothing shows what AED 400K a month returned. *CEO, Teammate, HM (3/7).*
6. **"…scaling the business from early traction to market leadership in its category."** An unproven claim with no category named. Signature Technology also has no location. *HM, CEO, Rival, HM day-one (4/7).*
7. **Earlier roles have no content.** "Performance Marketing Manager — Fremme… Oct 2020 – Jun 2022" covers almost two years in the core skill with nothing shown, and Vahitra was 8 months. There is no evidence before 2022, and some dates are years only. *Screen, HM day-one, HM (3/7).*
8. **Little on collaboration or people.** "Restructured the entire marketing function within the first quarter" says nothing about the team. "Partnered with sales…" has no result. Sales' wins are framed as marketing-only, and there's nothing on developing the team of 11. *Teammate (1/7, high impact for a leadership role).*
9. **"MBA — Completed Coursework"** has no date and reads as hiding an unfinished degree. *Rival, Screen, HM (3/7).*
10. **Missing keywords and seniority signals:** no AI, no "GCC", no Arabic (can't be added unless true), and "Go-to-Market" appears only in the competencies list. *Screen, Recruiter, Rival, HM day-one (4/7).*
11. **Context missing from numbers:** no timeframe for the 72K→2.45M traffic, no spend or period for "ROAS of 20", and "Cost Per Order" is unexplained for a SaaS company. *Recruiter, HM (2/7).*
12. **Formatting:** older roles merge title, employer and location on one line, title formats are inconsistent ("Lead / Executive"), "MOEngage" is misspelled, and "CRM Platforms" and "Marketing Automation" are listed as tools. *Screen (1/7).*
